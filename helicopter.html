Lunch · Brunch · Dinner

The experience pairs a flight around the valley with three courses and drinks at our signature restaurant on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center.

Larger helicopters are available for groups up to five. The flight can be paired with a three-course lunch, brunch, or dinner, so the timing works around your day rather than the other way around. Have a bigger party? We can build a custom package with multiple helicopters and separate flight plans.

No matter the occasion or the size of your group, Volanti can craft an experience to meet your needs and memories you will never forget.

“You check in at the restaurant, we walk you down to the private runway, and the whole evening is already in motion.”

What the experience includes

Manifest · 04 items
  1. 01

    The Flight

    A customized flight around the valley for two, taking in the city, the desert and the McDowell Mountains from the air. Larger helicopters seat groups up to five.

  2. 02

    $150 Dining Credit

    A $150 credit toward food and beverage for your visit. You and your guests order from our full menu, choosing whatever you like rather than a fixed plate.

  3. 03

    Your Flight Time

    Select the flight time that works best for you. Please arrive at least 15 minutes ahead of your flight reservation and check in at the restaurant. That gives us time to get you settled and down to our private runway so the flight takes off on schedule.

  4. 04

    Custom Group Packages

    For larger parties we build custom packages with multiple helicopters and coordinated flight plans, plus private event space inside the restaurant for the dining half of the evening.

Before you book

Flight times are subject to change due to inclement weather, helicopter availability and similar conditions outside our control.

Due to the nature of the experience and the booking times, refunds are not available. Rescheduling is subject to availability and at the discretion of Volanti and the pilots.

Questions about a date or a larger party? Call 480.657.2426 or email hello@volantiscottsdale.com.
