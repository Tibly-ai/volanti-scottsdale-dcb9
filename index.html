01 · The Room

Second floor, window seats, one very good view.

Volanti Scottsdale sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, looking straight out at Scottsdale Airport with the McDowell Mountains on the horizon. It is a full-service airport, but the Business Center opens right into the restaurant. You never need a ticket or a security checkpoint to get to your table.

The kitchen works from scratch and leans on fresh, seasonal produce, which is why the menus shift through the year. Lunch runs Tuesday through Friday, dinner Tuesday through Saturday, and brunch takes over both weekend mornings. Happy hour lives at the bar, and there is a kids menu for the smallest passengers.

Parking is a large covered lot directly across from the building, shared with the rest of the Business Center. Coming from Scottsdale Quarter? It is a 6 minute walk.
