Before You Arrive

Three things worth knowing

  1. 01

    Patio Seating

    We do not take exclusive reservations for the patio. It is a high demand area with very limited space, so availability cannot be guaranteed in advance. To keep it fair, patio seating is limited to an hour and a half for parties of four or fewer, and two hours for parties of five or more. Add a patio preference to your booking and we will do our best when you arrive. If nothing is open at your time, let the host know and we can add you to the patio wait list.

  2. 02

    No Security Line

    You do not need to be flying anywhere. We sit on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center overlooking Scottsdale Airport, and the Business Center has direct access to the restaurant. No boarding pass, no security checkpoint, no ticket. Walk in, take the stairs or the elevator, and the runway is right there through the glass.

  3. 03

    Parking & Walking

    There is a large, covered parking lot directly across from our building. It is shared by the whole Aviation Business Center, including Volanti Scottsdale, City of Scottsdale Airport staff, our venue, and United States Customs. Coming from Scottsdale Quarter? It is roughly a six minute walk.

A scratch kitchen, so a little planning helps

Our kitchen cooks from scratch and leans on fresh, seasonal product, which means the menu moves and the line works to order. We are not able to accommodate to-go orders. If you are short on time, bar seats are the move: quick bites, full pours, same view of the runway.

Bringing kids? There is a kids menu at lunch, dinner and brunch. Meeting friends after work? Happy hour runs at the bar. And if you keep a wine locker with us, your reservation gets first call on patio availability and event seating.
