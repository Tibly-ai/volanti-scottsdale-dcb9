The Program

Your wine, your locker, your table

Do you have a passion for wine and spirits, love early access to the newest offerings, or simply enjoy having a personalized supply of wine at your fingertips every time you visit your favorite restaurant? Volanti's wine locker program is the experience for you.

Our in-house sommelier will help you curate a wine locker selection that will be stored on site for your exclusive use. Reserve your locker today and enjoy your next glass of wine with friends and family while overlooking the runway at Scottsdale Airport and the McDowell Mountains.

We designed the program to complement and build on the standard wine cellar programs of the traditional locker, name plate, and storage space. Members receive the space and a discount on bottle purchase price, plus a set of perks that reach well past storage.

No security checkpoint

The Aviation Business Center has direct access to the restaurant. You never need a boarding pass to reach your locker.

Parking across the street

A large covered lot sits across from the building, a short six minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter.
