Hundred Dollar Hamburger

Ask any pilot about the hundred dollar hamburger and you will get a grin. It is the excuse to preflight, file, fly somewhere with a decent kitchen, eat, and fly home. The burger costs what a burger costs. The rest goes in the tanks.

Volanti was built for that kind of afternoon. We sit on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center, directly above the ramp, with the McDowell Mountains behind the runway. Park in the covered lot across from the building, walk up, and you are at the table. No boarding pass, no security line, no terminal maze. If you flew in, taxi over and come up the stairs.

Our kitchen is a scratch kitchen, so the menu follows the season rather than a freezer. If you are on a tight turn, the bar is the fastest seat in the house for a quick bite before you push back out. If you have the whole day, take a window table and watch departures roll.

The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, The Palmeraie Opening Party

In August 2022 our team was part of the opening party for The Ritz-Carlton Paradise Valley, The Palmeraie. A night off property is a different kind of service: everything is prepped here, packed, driven, and plated on someone else's floor, on someone else's clock.

It is the same muscle we use for jet catering and private events. Our kitchen cooks to order, our service team reads a room, and the sommelier pours to match whatever the evening turns into. Nights like the Palmeraie opening are the reason we keep both sides of the house sharp.

If you are planning something of your own, from a jet departure order to a full buyout of the dining room and patio above the runway, start a conversation with us and we will build the plan around your timing.
