The Arrivals Board

07 Entries · All Services

  • 01

    Do you take to-go orders?

    Our restaurant team is unable to accommodate to-go orders. Volanti operates a scratch kitchen that prioritizes fresh, seasonal offerings, and with those constraints plus a full room of guests to look after, we keep everything on-property. If you are short on time, bar seats are open for a quick bite.

  • 02

    Do I need to be flying out or clear security?

    No. We sit on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center overlooking Scottsdale Airport. It is a full-service airport, but the Business Center has direct access to the restaurant, so you never need a boarding pass and never pass through a security checkpoint. Walk in, take the stairs or elevator, and the runway is right there.

  • 03

    Where do I park?

    There is a large, covered parking lot across from our building. It serves the entire Aviation Business Center, including Volanti, the City of Scottsdale Airport staff, our venue, and United States Customs, so allow a moment at busier hours. Scottsdale Quarter is a six minute walk away if you would rather come on foot.

  • 04

    Can I reserve the patio?

    Seating limit 1:30 / 2:00

    We do not take exclusive reservations for patio seating. The patio is a high demand area with very limited space, so we cannot guarantee availability in advance. To keep it moving for everyone, patio seating is limited to an hour and a half for parties of four or less, and two hours for parties of five or more.

    We can add a patio preference to your reservation and we will do our best to seat you there when you arrive. If nothing is open at your reservation time, let the hostess know and she can add you to the patio wait list while you are seated inside.

  • 05

    Is there a kids menu?

    Yes. We keep a kids menu for lunch and dinner service, and a separate kids brunch menu on Saturday and Sunday. You can read both on the menus page before you come in.

  • 06

    What happens if weather grounds my helicopter tour?

    Flight times are subject to change for inclement weather, helicopter availability and similar factors. Because of the way the experience is booked, refunds are not available. Rescheduling is subject to availability and at the discretion of Volanti and the pilots. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your flight time and check in at the restaurant. Full details are on the helicopter tour page.

  • 07

    Do you sell gift cards and host private events?

    Both. Gift cards are available for dining and for our experiences, and our venue hosts private events of most sizes. Jet catering and the wine locker program run out of the same kitchen and cellar. Tell us the date and the headcount using the form below and we will send options.

Runway view from the second floor

Still need a person?

The host stand answers the phone during service and reads email between shifts. For anything that needs a real conversation, a wedding-sized party, a catering order for an early departure, a locker you want to start filling, call us and we will walk through it.

Careers
We hire for the floor, the bar, the kitchen and events. Send a short note about the shifts you want along with your resume to hello@volantiscottsdale.com and the management team will follow up.
Large parties
Groups, buyouts and wine dinners are arranged by hand rather than through the online book. Start with the form below or call 480.657.2426.
Jet catering
Orders for departing aircraft come out of the same scratch kitchen. Give us your tail number, departure time and any dietary notes, and we will build the order around them.
Wine lockers
Our in-house sommelier curates each locker and members get patio reservation access, first priority on events and wine dinners, and limited release bottles. Details on the wine lockers page.
