The Manifest

Five ways to spend the day with us

01 Lunch · Brunch · Dinner

Helicopter Tour

A customized helicopter experience for two, paired with three courses and drinks at the restaurant, then a flight around the valley for the city views. Larger helicopters seat groups up to five, and bigger parties can be flown on a custom plan with multiple aircraft and flight paths.

  • The flight itself, at a time you choose
  • A $150 credit toward food and beverage, spent anywhere on the full menu
  • Check in at the restaurant 15 minutes early so we can get you down to the private ramp on time

Flight times can change with weather and helicopter availability. Because of how the experience is booked, refunds are not available. Rescheduling depends on availability and is at the discretion of Volanti and the pilots.

Book the flight
02 Membership

Wine Lockers

For anyone with a passion for wine and spirits, an appetite for early access to new releases, or a simple preference for having their own bottles waiting. Our in-house sommelier helps curate a selection that is stored on site for your exclusive use.

Members get more than a name plate and a shelf: a discount on bottle purchases, exclusive access to patio reservations, first priority on events and wine dinners, after-hours offers, discounts on our venue, and limited release wines and spirits that rarely make it to a list.

Reserve a locker
03 By Request

Jet Catering

We are already at the airport, which makes the kitchen a short walk from your aircraft. Our team builds catering orders from the same scratch kitchen that runs the dining room, with fresh, seasonal ingredients and a menu shaped around your departure time and passenger count.

Call ahead with your tail number, timing and preferences, and we will confirm the details with your crew.

04 Venue

Private Events

Our venue sits on the second floor of the Aviation Business Center with the runway on one side and the McDowell Mountains on the other. Guests arrive without passing a security checkpoint and park in the covered lot across from the building, a six minute walk from Scottsdale Quarter.

Birthdays, company dinners, wine dinners and receptions are all built to order with the kitchen. Wine locker members receive discounts on the venue and other event accommodations. Tell us the date and the headcount and we will take it from there.
